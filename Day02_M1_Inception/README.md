## 📌 Overview

The third session of Day 1 focused on practical familiarization with the OpenLane physical design environment. The lab activities covered directory exploration, design preparation, reviewing project files, understanding the OpenLane project structure and introductory design characterization.

## 💻 Session 3 — Get Familiar with the OpenLane Environment

### 🧪 Lab 1 — OpenLane Directory Exploration

**Objective:** Explore the OpenLane directory and become familiar with the environment used for physical design implementation.

**Activities**

* Navigate through the OpenLane directory.
* Explore the available folders and files.
* Identify the basic structure of the working environment.
* Understand where design-related resources are organized.

**Learning outcome:** Gained initial familiarity with the OpenLane directory structure and its working environment.

![OpenLane Directory Exploration](../../assets/day-01/lab/lab-01-openlane-directory.png)

### 🧪 Lab 2 — Design Preparation

**Objective:** Understand the initial design preparation process and the files required to begin working with a design.

**Activities**

* Explore the design preparation steps.
* Familiarize yourself with the design's directory and configuration structure.
* Identify the resources required for the next stages of implementation.

**Learning outcome:** Developed an initial understanding of design preparation within the OpenLane environment.

![Design Preparation](../../assets/day-01/lab/lab-02-design-preparation.png)

### 🧪 Lab 3 — Review Files After Design Preparation

**Objective:** Review the files and directory structure generated or made available after design preparation.

**Activities**

* Examine the prepared design files.
* Explore relevant directories.
* Review the relationship between design inputs and the preparation workflow.

**Learning outcome:** Improved familiarity with design files and the organization of the implementation environment.

![Design File Review](../../assets/day-01/lab/lab-03-file-review.png)

### 🧪 Lab 4 — OpenLane Project Git Familiarization

**Objective:** Explore the OpenLane project and understand its organization and workflow.

**Activities**

* Familiarize yourself with the OpenLane project structure.
* Explore relevant project resources.
* Understand how the project files support the implementation flow.

**Learning outcome:** Gained familiarity with the OpenLane project and its associated resources.

![OpenLane Project](../../assets/day-01/lab/lab-04-openlane-project.png)

### 🧪 Lab 5 — Steps to Characterize a Design

**Objective:** Become familiar with the introductory steps involved in design characterization.

**Activities**

* Explore the design characterization workflow.
* Understand the importance of design information and characterization.
* Identify how characterization relates to the overall physical design process.

**Learning outcome:** Developed an introductory understanding of design characterization and its role in VLSI implementation.

![Design Characterization](../../assets/day-01/lab/lab-05-characterization.png)
