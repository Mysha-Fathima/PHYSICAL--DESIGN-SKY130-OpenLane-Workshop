## 📌 Overview

The third session of Day 2 introduces the fundamentals of standard-cell design and characterization. The lab topics cover the inputs required for a cell design flow, circuit design, layout design and typical cell characterization.

These activities provide an introduction to the relationship between circuit-level implementation, physical layout and the electrical characteristics of standard cells.

---

# 💻 Session 3 — Cell Design and Characterization

## 🧪 Lab 1 — Inputs for Cell Design Flow

**Objective:** Understand the essential inputs required to begin a standard-cell design flow.

**Topics Covered**

* Introduction to the cell design flow.
* Identification of design specifications and requirements.
* Understanding the relationship between circuit design and physical implementation.
* Introduction to technology and design-related inputs.

**Technical Description**

Cell design begins with defining the required functionality and electrical characteristics of the target cell. The design flow uses circuit specifications, technology information and design constraints to guide circuit development, layout creation and characterization.

**Learning Outcome**

Developed an introductory understanding of the inputs and requirements involved in a standard-cell design flow.

## 🧪 Lab 2 — Circuit Design Step

**Objective:** Explore the circuit-level design stage involved in developing a standard cell.

**Topics Covered**

* Introduction to transistor-level circuit design.
* Understanding circuit topology.
* CMOS circuit implementation fundamentals.
* Relationship between transistor sizing and electrical behavior.

**Technical Description**

The circuit design stage defines how transistors are connected to implement the required logic function. Circuit topology and transistor sizing influence important characteristics such as power consumption, delay and drive strength.

**Learning Outcome**

Gained an introductory understanding of transistor-level circuit design and its role in standard-cell development.

## 🧪 Lab 3 — Layout Design Step

**Objective:** Understand how a circuit schematic is translated into a physical layout suitable for integrated-circuit fabrication.

**Topics Covered**

* Introduction to physical layout design.
* Transistor and interconnect organization.
* Layout design considerations.
* Relationship between circuit schematic and physical layout.
* Introduction to design-rule considerations.

**Technical Description**

The layout design stage converts the circuit topology into physical geometries according to the target process technology. The placement of devices, routing of interconnections and adherence to technology-specific design rules are important for creating a manufacturable layout.

**Learning Outcome**

Understood the fundamental relationship between circuit design and physical layout, along with the importance of technology-specific layout constraints.

## 🧪 Lab 4 — Typical Characterization

**Objective:** Understand the purpose of typical cell characterization and the electrical information required to describe standard-cell behavior.

**Topics Covered**

* Introduction to standard-cell characterization.
* Timing characteristics.
* Power characteristics.
* Input and output transition behavior.
* Cell performance under defined operating conditions.

**Technical Description**

Cell characterization evaluates the electrical behavior of a standard cell under specified operating conditions. The resulting timing and power information can be represented in library models used by digital implementation and static timing analysis tools.

**Learning Outcome**

Developed an introductory understanding of standard-cell characterization and its importance in timing-driven digital implementation.
